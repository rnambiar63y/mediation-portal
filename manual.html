1. Overview & Role-Based Access

The Mediation Portal provides role-isolated access to case records. Upon opening the portal, select your respective role on the login screen and enter your authorized PIN:

Mediator (മധ്യസ്ഥൻ): Select your name from the dropdown and enter your mediator PIN.
Accesses only the cases explicitly assigned to your mediation docket.
Advocate (അഡ്വക്കേറ്റ്): Select Advocate Login and enter your authorized PIN.
Accesses only the cases where you are listed as the Petitioner's or Respondent's advocate.
Party / Client (കക്ഷി): Enter the specific Case Number (e.g., OP 1117/2026) and your party PIN.
Accesses exclusively the details, status, and scheduled dates for your specific matter.

2. Case Dashboard & Status Tracking

Once authenticated, the dashboard displays your authorized case list:

  • Case Identity: Displays Folio Number, Case Type (e.g., Family Court, JMFC), and Parties involved.
  • Status Tag: Clearly shows whether the matter is Pending, Settled, or Referred.
  • Key Dates: Highlights the Reference Date, Mediation Return Date, and subsequent Court Posting Date.

3. Updating & Recording Progress

Mediators can update case parameters in real time:

  • Status Update: Update the status as mediation progresses (e.g., transitioning from Pending to Settled).
  • Date Scheduling: Record upcoming return dates and court postings.
  • Voice Dictation & Notes: Utilize the integrated microphone button (Voice Dictation) in the notes section to dictate proceedings, mediator observations, or advocate notes hands-free, ensuring accurate and efficient documentation.

4. Printing & Documentation

At any stage, clicking the Print / Export Case Details button formats the current case data into a clean, A4 landscape-friendly statement suitable for official records and court submission.
