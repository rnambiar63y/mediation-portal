<div class="section">

<h2>1. Overview &amp; Role-Based Access</h2>

<p>The Mediation Portal provides role-isolated access to case records. Upon opening the portal, select your respective role on the login screen and enter your authorized PIN:</p>

<div class="pin-box">

<strong>Mediator (മധ്യസ്ഥൻ):</strong> Select your name from the dropdown and enter your mediator PIN.<br>

<span class="note">Accesses only the cases explicitly assigned to your mediation docket.</span>

</div>

<div class="pin-box">

<strong>Advocate (അഡ്വക്കേറ്റ്):</strong> Select Advocate Login and enter your authorized PIN.<br>

<span class="note">Accesses only the cases where you are listed as the Petitioner's or Respondent's advocate.</span>

</div>


<div class="pin-box">

<strong>Party / Client (കക്ഷി):</strong> Enter the specific Case Number (e.g., OP 1117/2026) and your party PIN.<br>

<span class="note">Accesses exclusively the details, status, and scheduled dates for your specific matter.</span>

</div>

</div>


<div class="section">

<h2>2. Case Dashboard &amp; Status Tracking</h2>

<p>Once authenticated, the dashboard displays your authorized case list:</p>

<ul>

<li><strong>Case Identity:</strong> Displays Folio Number, Case Type (e.g., Family Court, JMFC), and Parties involved.</li>

<li><strong>Status Tag:</strong> Clearly shows whether the matter is <code>Pending</code>, <code>Settled</code>, or <code>Referred</code>.</li>

<li><strong>Key Dates:</strong> Highlights the <code>Reference Date</code>, <code>Mediation Return Date</code>, and subsequent <code>Court Posting Date</code>.</li>

</ul>

</div>


<div class="section">

<h2>3. Updating &amp; Recording Progress</h2>

<p>Mediators can update case parameters in real time:</p>

<ul>

<li><strong>Status Update:</strong> Update the status as mediation progresses (e.g., transitioning from Pending to Settled).</li>

<li><strong>Date Scheduling:</strong> Record upcoming return dates and court postings.</li>

<li><strong>Voice Dictation &amp; Notes:</strong> Utilize the integrated microphone button (Voice Dictation) in the notes section to dictate proceedings, mediator observations, or advocate notes hands-free, ensuring accurate and efficient documentation.</li>

</ul>

</div>


<div class="section">

<h2>4. Printing &amp; Documentation</h2>

<p>At any stage, clicking the <strong>Print / Export Case Details</strong> button formats the current case data into a clean, A4 landscape-friendly statement suitable for official records and court submission.</p>

</div>
