General Portal Access

1. General Portal Access & Navigation

Select your authorized role card on the home screen (Admin, Mediator, Advocate, or Party / Client), choose your preferred language (English, Malayalam, or Kannada), and sign in. Typed passcodes and PINs are displayed clearly on screen so you can verify your entry before logging in (use the Hide button if someone is nearby).
