📖 User Manual — Mediation Center Portal

1. How to Share This Application

  • Click the 🔗 Share App button in the top navigation bar to copy the live portal link (https://rnambiar63y.github.io/mediation-portal/) and paste it directly into WhatsApp, SMS, or Email.
  • No installation or login password is required—it works on Android, iPhone, tablets, and desktop browsers.

2. Switching User Roles (Top-Right Menu)

  • Mediator: View assigned cases, update next mediation posting dates, record session notes, and mark matters as Settled or Not Settled.
  • Center Admin (Register): Enter new court referrals using + New Court Referral Entry and assign mediators.
  • Advocate / Party / Client: View case posting dates and check mediation status.

3. Managing Case Folios & Court Return Reports

  • Add a New Case: Click + New Court Referral Entry, enter the Folio Number, Case No., Court Name, Parties, and Mediator, then click Save Referral.
  • Update Posting / Generate Report: Click directly on any case card in the register to update the next posting date, change status, or print/save a Court Return Report.
Note on Data Privacy & Storage: Entries added in this browser are stored locally on your device for privacy and offline speed. Other users opening the shared link on their devices will see the standard template register unless data is exported or connected to a shared backend.
